Why 3A3D exists

Great machines
deserve a closer look.

3A3D began as a way to bring a personal interest in cars into a web project. It brings together manufacturers, classic designs and performance icons in one place.

The goal is simple: make discovery enjoyable. Give every vehicle room to breathe, make specifications easy to read, and build an experience that works just as well on a phone as on a desktop.

This is an independent student showcase, not a commercial dealership. Vehicles are presented for exploration; there are no sales, inventory claims or booking services.

01 / DISCOVER

Design with character.

A restrained black-and-orange identity keeps the focus on the vehicles.

02 / EXPLORE

Details within reach.

Searchable collections and readable specifications make browsing purposeful.

03 / BUILD

Simple by design.

Semantic HTML, shared CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework required.
