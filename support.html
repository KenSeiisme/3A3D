Good to know

A few answers.

Is 3A3D a real dealership? +

No. This is an independent student portfolio project. The collection is for exploration and does not represent vehicles available for purchase.

Can I buy a car or book a test drive? +

There are no sales, payment or booking services on this website.

Where do the specifications come from? +

The specifications were carried over from the original project and consolidated across pages. They are reference information, not independently verified manufacturer specifications. Output and equipment can vary by model year, market and variant.

Why is the Initial D spotlight focused on the AE86? +

The AE86 association is confirmed by the series publisher. Other models are kept in the general collection until their exact variants can be checked. The displayed specifications do not describe a modified story car.

How can I report a mistake? +

Email the creator with the model name, the detail to correct and a reliable reference. Feedback on accessibility or the site experience is welcome too.

Does browsing require an account? +

No. You can explore every vehicle without signing in. Filtering happens in your browser; the site has no account or reservation system.
